# How to use PGYM

PGYM is proof of gym. You log your sets, take one photo at the end, and get a card with the date, the time, what you did and a proof number. This guide walks through every part of the app, one screen per step.

Each step names the screen it happens on, so you can follow along on your phone.

## 1. Set a goal

The goal is how many days a week you train. The streak counts weeks, not days, so rest days never break it.

1. **Sign in.** Choose Continue with Apple, Continue with Google, or Use email instead.
   *Screen A1, Welcome. [Screenshot to add]*
2. **Give your year of birth.** Apple and Google don't share your age, so PGYM asks once. It decides whether you can turn on the AI score later (18 and over only).
   *Screen A5, Year of birth. [Screenshot to add]*
3. **Pick your number.** "How many days a week?" Choose 2 to 6. PGYM shows the sentence back to you, for example "I will train 4 days a week". Tap Lock it in.
   *Screen B1, Weekly goal. [Screenshot to add]*
4. **Pick your days and a time.** Turn on as many days as your goal and choose one time. Your reminder uses both.
   *Screen B2, Gym days and time. [Screenshot to add]*
5. **Choose about reminders.** PGYM sends one reminder on each gym day, and none once the session is logged. Allow notifications, or skip.
   *Screen B3, Reminders. [Screenshot to add]*
6. **Check your units.** kg or lb, set from your phone's region. One tap changes it.
   *Screen B5, Units. [Screenshot to add]*

To change the goal later, go to Profile, then Goal and schedule. A new goal starts the following week, never the current one.
*Screen F4, Goal and schedule. [Screenshot to add]*

## 2. Build a routine

A routine is a named list of exercises with target sets and reps, for example "Push A".

1. **Start from a template or from scratch.** During setup, pick Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower or Full body, or tap Build my own.
   *Screen B4, First routine. [Screenshot to add]*
2. **See all your routines.** Profile, then Routines. Drag to reorder; the order decides which routine is "next up". Tap Add for a new one.
   *Screen F2, Routines. [Screenshot to add]*
3. **Edit a routine.** Give it a name, add exercises in order, and set target sets and reps for each.
   *Screen F3, Routine editor. [Screenshot to add]*

## 3. Log a session

1. **Start.** The Today tab shows your week and the Next up card. Tap Start session.
   *Screen C1, Today. [Screenshot to add]*
   On a rest day the card says "Rest day" with a Train anyway link (screen C2). When your week is done, the card offers a bonus session (screen C3).
2. **Pick the routine.** The next one is at the top. You can choose another, or an empty session.
   *Screen D1, Pick routine. [Screenshot to add]*
3. **Log each set.** Last session's weights are already filled in. Tap the check when a set is done. Change the weight or reps first if they were different. The rest timer starts on its own, and a new best is flagged.
   *Screen D2, Active session. [Screenshot to add]*
4. **Add an exercise.** Search, pick from recent, or browse by muscle group.
   *Screen D3, Add exercise. [Screenshot to add]*
5. **Change one set.** Long-press a set to mark it warm-up or working, add an effort rating or a note, or delete it. Warm-up sets don't count toward total weight lifted.
   *Screen D4, Set detail. [Screenshot to add]*
6. **Leaving early.** Swiping down asks first. Save and finish without proof keeps your sets but does not count toward the week. Discard deletes the session.
   *Screen D5, Leave session? [Screenshot to add]*

No signal at the gym is fine. Sets are saved on your phone as you log them and sync later.

## 4. Take the proof

A session counts when it has at least one logged set and one photo.

1. **Finish.** Tap Finish and take proof at the bottom of the active session.
   *Screen D2, Active session. [Screenshot to add]*
2. **Allow the camera.** The first time only, PGYM explains why it needs the camera before iOS asks.
   *Screen D6, Camera permission. [Screenshot to add]*
3. **Take one photo.** Front or back camera, with an optional 3 second timer. There is no library button: the photo has to be taken now, in the app. That is what makes it proof.
   *Screen D7, Proof camera. [Screenshot to add]*
4. **Make the card.** Pick a filter (Iron, Chalk, Plate or Raw), choose which numbers show on the card, and choose 4:5 for a feed or 9:16 for a story. Retake replaces the photo. Tap Stamp it.
   *Screen D8, Proof card editor. [Screenshot to add]*
5. **Wait for the stamp.** The server receives proof metadata, sets the time and your proof number, and the photo stays on your iPhone. Offline, the card is saved with a "pending" stamp and gets its server time when you're back online.
   *Screen D9, Stamping. [Screenshot to add]*
6. **See what changed.** Your week moves, new bests show, and so does the total weight lifted. Open the stamped workout's detail page to request optional Pro AI analysis.
   *Screen D11, Session complete. [Screenshot to add]*

Proof numbers count up and are never reused. #042 means the forty-second time you showed up. One session gets one proof.

## 5. Share it

1. **Share from the end of the session.** Tap Share proof on the Session complete screen.
   *Screen D11, Session complete. [Screenshot to add]*
2. **Pick where it goes.** The iOS share sheet opens with the card image. Send it to any app, or Save to Photos.
   *Screen D12, Share. [Screenshot to add]*
3. **Choose your card overlay.** Before tapping Done on the completed card, choose Workout or AI scores. Workout shows your sets, weight lifted and time. AI scores shows the six dimensions and overall score after you generate analysis. The same two choices are available when you open an older session from your proof wall and tap Share again.
   *Screen E3, Session detail. [Screenshot to add]*

The workout overlay does not show AI scores. The AI score overlay does not show workout statistics; coaching stays on the detail page. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

## 6. Read your progress

1. **The Progress tab.** Proof count, streak, your score if it's on, a 12-week chart against your goal, a preview of your proof wall, and your lifts with their bests.
   *Screen E1, Progress. [Screenshot to add]*
2. **The proof wall.** Every card in a grid, newest first, grouped by month.
   *Screen E2, Proof wall. [Screenshot to add]*
3. **One session.** The card, every set, the totals, and that day's score and tip.
   *Screen E3, Session detail. [Screenshot to add]*
4. **One lift.** Your best set, estimated one-rep max over time, and every session's sets for that exercise. The estimate uses the Epley formula: weight x (1 + reps / 30).
   *Screen E4, Lift detail. [Screenshot to add]*

How the streak works: hit your weekly number and the week locks. Every 4 weeks you hit earns a pass week, used on its own when a week falls short. Weeks start on Monday in your time zone.

## 7. Turn the AI score on or off

AI analysis is optional, for eligible adults with Pro. It estimates six visible dimensions and a separate overall fitness and muscle score from 0 to 100. It adds three coaching sentences and one encouraging sentence. Results stay private in your history unless you choose a score-sharing card layout.

1. **Turn it on.** On the completed card, before tapping Done, or from a stamped workout's detail page, tap Get AI analysis and coaching. If you are not subscribed, PGYM shows the Pro plans. Before the first analysis, PGYM shows what is sent and who sees it. Tap Turn on, or Not now. It is off until you say yes, and only available to eligible adults.
   *Screen D10, Physique consent. [Screenshot to add]*
2. **See it over time.** Open Progress, then Score history. Pick one metric or Overall fitness and muscle from the menu; read each session point, its seven-day average and your personal bests. Tap What do the six scores mean? for descriptions. The workout detail page shows that day's coaching.
   *Screen E5, Score history. [Screenshot to add]*
3. **Turn it off.** Profile, then Privacy and data, then switch Physique analysis off. Turning it off also deletes your stored scores.
   *Screen F6, Privacy and data. [Screenshot to add]*

Some photos cannot be scored, including partial-body images, more than one person, or poor light. The session still counts. Every score is an AI estimate from a photo, not medical advice. For a repeatable capture routine and guidance on reading the charts, see [Measure the work. See the change.](/blog/measure-the-work-see-the-change).

## 8. Delete your data

1. **Delete one session.** Open it from the proof wall and tap Delete. The photo, the card and the session are removed, and your counts are recalculated.
   *Screen E3, Session detail. [Screenshot to add]*
2. **Delete all photos, or export your data.** Profile, then Privacy and data.
   *Screen F6, Privacy and data. [Screenshot to add]*
3. **Delete your account.** Privacy and data, then Delete account. PGYM lists what will be deleted. Type DELETE to confirm. Your photos, cards, sessions and sign-in are removed.
   *Screen F7, Delete account. [Screenshot to add]*

No longer have the app? Use the form on [Delete your account](/delete-account).
